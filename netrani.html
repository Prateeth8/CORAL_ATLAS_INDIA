NDWI — Normalized Difference Water Index

Computed as (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR) using Sentinel-2 bands B3 and B8. Highlights water bodies and submerged reef structures. Values range from −1 (land) to +1 (open water).

Coral Spectral Index

Derived from Sentinel-2 red-edge and NIR bands to discriminate live coral from algae, sediment, and bleached substrate. Higher values indicate greater live coral reflectance signature.
